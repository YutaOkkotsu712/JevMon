# Switching to clear effects

`switching.onSuccessfulSwitchOut` describes the outgoing Pokémon once per payload, including minimal detail. It is conditional on a successful **ordinary switch**, not an instruction to switch. The server's legal choices, trapping warnings, incoming damage and hazards still apply. Forced replacements after fainting do not receive a relief bonus. Baton Pass transfers are not represented as ordinary switches.

Covered benefits: Leech Seed, Salt Cure, Ghost-type Curse, Nightmare, confusion, attraction, Yawn, Perish Song, Encore, Disable, Taunt, Torment, Heal Block, Embargo, Throat Chop, Syrup Bomb, binding and Octolock; resetting negative stages and Choice locks; Natural Cure, Regenerator and Toxic counter reset. Known indirect damage prevention is respected. Drain percentages approximate whole-HP rounding; they are damage avoided by the outgoing Pokémon, not healing or damage avoided by its replacement. Unknown Binding Band is explicitly flagged.

Costs and qualifications: positive stages and Substitute are lost, along with effects such as Aqua Ring, Ingrain, Focus Energy and Magnet Rise. Temporary typing and other mixed changes are described without automatically valuing them as benefits. Burn, ordinary poison, Toxic, paralysis, sleep and freeze persist unless a known curing ability applies. Toxic's counter reset does not cure poison. Unrecognised observed effects are retained as `effectsNotPriced`; their value is not fabricated.

The cycle guard allows switches with an identified relief benefit. It does not require a switch or bypass other entry checks. Repeated use of a move while removable residual damage drains the active Pokémon adds a warning to reconsider the exchange. This warning does not claim to have simulated future opponent actions or measured net progress.

Protocol fixes: binding is tracked from its `-activate` event and removed by the matching `-end`; Perish Song countdown updates replace old counts. Residual calculations include Nightmare, Aqua Ring/Ingrain (including Big Root), known Binding Band and Heal Block.

Validation: targeted unit tests plus `SIMULATOR_DIR=/path/to/simulator npm exec -- node scripts/test-switch-relief-simulator.mjs`. The simulator checks 12 effect removals through ordinary switching and verifies persistent sleep. Offline reconstruction of battle 2686022833, turn 17, now reports clearing Leech Seed, avoiding 12.5% outgoing max-HP drain, persistent sleep and the repeated Throat Chop warning. All 34 reconstructed decisions fit the 24,000-byte budget (5 reduced, 29 minimal; peak 23,949 bytes). The 215-test suite passed after rerunning the loopback UI test with the required bind permission; the final focused run passed 22 tests. This is a feature replay, not a new Jev decision or proof of improved win rate.
